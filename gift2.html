Formatting Instructions

Use **numeric numbering** for questions and **non-numeric bullets** for answer options.

Example format:

1. You are not sick.
   i. hope
   ii. tired
   iii. well
   iv. remember

Ensure that questions start with a **number followed by a dot (e.g., 1.)**, and options should be **indented and use non-numeric bullets (i, ii, iii, etc.)**.
